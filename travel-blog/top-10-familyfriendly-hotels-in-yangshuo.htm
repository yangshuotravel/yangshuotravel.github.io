Top 10 Family-Friendly Hotels in Yangshuo

Home / Travel Blog / Blog Details

Yangshuo, a dreamlike destination in southern China’s Guangxi region, has long been a magnet for backpackers, climbers, and photographers. But in recent years, this karst mountain paradise has quietly transformed into one of the most welcoming family vacation spots in Asia. The secret? A perfect storm of gentle rivers, safe cycling paths, farm-to-table dining, and a new wave of hotels that cater specifically to parents traveling with children. Whether you are chasing the iconic Li River views, hoping to watch your kids feed water buffalo, or simply want a pool with a view while the little ones nap, Yangshuo delivers. Below is a curated list of the top 10 family-friendly hotels in Yangshuo, each offering something unique for travelers with children.

1. Yangshuo Mountain Retreat: The Original Family Haven

Perched on the banks of the Yulong River, Yangshuo Mountain Retreat has been a favorite among families for over a decade. This property strikes a delicate balance between rustic charm and modern convenience. The rooms are spacious, with many offering direct river views and private balconies where you can sip tea while watching bamboo rafts drift by.

Why families love it: The hotel provides free bicycles for all ages, including child seats and small bikes for toddlers. The on-site restaurant serves a dedicated kids’ menu featuring mild Chinese dishes and Western comfort food like pasta and burgers. The real highlight, however, is the shallow, slow-moving river section right in front of the hotel. Children can safely wade, splash, and try their hand at catching tiny fish with nets provided by the staff.

Pro tip: Book the family suite on the ground floor. It opens directly onto a small garden where your kids can run around while you keep an eye on them from the terrace.

2. Banyan Tree Yangshuo: Luxury Without the Stress

If you are looking for a resort that anticipates every family need before you even think of it, Banyan Tree Yangshuo is your answer. This sprawling property is set against the iconic karst peaks, with a private lake and lush gardens that feel like a world apart from the bustling West Street.

Why families love it: The resort runs a full kids’ club called the Little Rangers Club, open daily from 9 AM to 6 PM. Activities include Chinese calligraphy lessons, lantern making, cooking classes (where kids learn to make dumplings), and nature walks. Parents can enjoy the spa, the infinity pool, or a quiet dinner at the Chinese restaurant while the children are entertained by trained staff. The rooms are enormous, with separate living areas that can accommodate a crib or extra bed without feeling cramped.

Pro tip: Request a room in the villas section. The private plunge pools are a huge hit with kids, and the enclosed courtyard means you don’t have to worry about them wandering off.

3. Yangshuo Ancient Garden Boutique Hotel: A Cultural Immersion

Housed in a restored 200-year-old mansion in the heart of the old town, this boutique hotel offers something rare: a genuine cultural experience that is still comfortable for families. The architecture is classic Huizhou style, with carved wooden beams, stone courtyards, and koi ponds.

Why families love it: The hotel offers free guided walks through the old town, tailored for children. The guide points out hidden alleyways, local snacks, and even stops at a small shop where kids can try grinding rice flour by hand. The courtyard is a safe, enclosed space where children can play while parents enjoy a glass of wine from the small bar. The rooms are uniquely decorated, and kids love the four-poster beds and the antique trunks that double as storage.

Pro tip: The hotel does not have a full restaurant, but they partner with a nearby family-run eatery that delivers. Order the local beer fish—it’s mild and kids usually love the sweet-sour sauce.

4. Yangshuo Resort: The All-in-One Destination

Situated on a hill overlooking the Li River, Yangshuo Resort is a large property that feels like a small village. It has multiple pools, a tennis court, a spa, and several dining options. The resort is particularly popular with Chinese families, which means the staff is exceptionally experienced with children.

Why families love it: The resort offers a “Family Fun Package” that includes a guided bike tour along the river, a bamboo rafting trip (with life jackets for kids), and a Chinese cooking class. The pools are heated, and there is a separate shallow pool for toddlers. The breakfast buffet is a feast, with a dedicated station for kids offering mini pancakes, fruit skewers, and chocolate milk.

Pro tip: The resort is large, so request a room close to the main building if you have very young children. Otherwise, you might find yourself walking 10 minutes to the pool.

5. Hotel du Yangshuo: A Boutique Gem with a View

Perched on a cliffside, Hotel du Yangshuo offers some of the most dramatic views in the area. The infinity pool seems to merge with the karst peaks, creating a backdrop that is nothing short of breathtaking. The hotel is smaller and more intimate than the large resorts, which appeals to families who prefer a quieter atmosphere.

Why families love it: The hotel provides a free babysitting service for evening hours, allowing parents to enjoy a romantic dinner on the terrace. The rooms are designed with floor-to-ceiling windows, so even if your child is napping, you can still enjoy the view from the comfort of your room. The staff is incredibly attentive, often remembering children’s names and their favorite snacks.

Pro tip: The hotel is located on a steep hill, so if you are traveling with a stroller, be prepared for some steps. The staff will happily help carry the stroller up and down.

6. Li River Resort: Rustic Charm Meets Modern Safety

This family-run resort is located directly on the Li River, about 15 minutes from the center of Yangshuo. The property is built around a large, shaded garden with fruit trees, hammocks, and a small playground. The vibe is laid-back and unpretentious, perfect for families who want to escape the crowds.

Why families love it: The resort offers free kayaks and stand-up paddleboards for guests. The river section here is calm and shallow, making it safe for children to paddle around. The garden is a paradise for kids—they can pick oranges from the trees, chase butterflies, or simply roll down the gentle slopes. The restaurant serves home-style Chinese food, and the owner often joins families for dinner, sharing stories about local life.

Pro tip: Book the garden-view rooms on the ground floor. They have direct access to the lawn, so your kids can run out in the morning while you enjoy your coffee.

7. The Bamboo Retreat: Eco-Friendly Family Fun

For families who value sustainability and nature, The Bamboo Retreat is a hidden gem. This eco-lodge is built entirely from local materials, with bamboo walls, thatched roofs, and solar-powered heating. It is located in a small village about 30 minutes from Yangshuo, surrounded by rice paddies and karst peaks.

Why families love it: The retreat offers a “Farm to Table” experience where kids can help harvest vegetables, feed chickens, and even milk a goat. The cooking classes are hands-on, and children learn to make simple dishes like spring rolls and rice noodles. The rooms are basic but comfortable, and the lack of Wi-Fi (yes, intentionally) encourages families to spend time together. The staff organizes nightly bonfires with storytelling and marshmallow roasting.

Pro tip: Bring insect repellent. The eco-friendly setting means there are mosquitoes, especially near the rice paddies at dusk.

8. Yangshuo Tea Cozy: A Homestay with Heart

If you prefer a more personal experience, Yangshuo Tea Cozy is a charming homestay run by a local family. The property has only five rooms, each decorated with traditional Chinese textiles and local artwork. The hosts, Mr. and Mrs. Li, are known for their warmth and hospitality.

Why families love it: Mrs. Li offers free tea ceremonies for guests, and children are welcome to participate. She teaches kids how to brew tea properly and explains the different varieties. The homestay is located in a quiet alley, away from traffic, so children can play safely outside. The breakfast is homemade, with fresh bread, local honey, and seasonal fruits. The hosts also arrange private tours to nearby villages, tailoring the pace to your family’s needs.

Pro tip: Ask Mr. Li to take you on a night tour to see fireflies. He knows the best spots, and kids are always amazed by the glowing insects.

9. Yangshuo Secret Garden: A Hidden Paradise

Tucked away in a small village near the Moon Hill, Yangshuo Secret Garden is a collection of restored farmhouses turned into a boutique hotel. The property features multiple courtyards, a large pool, and a vegetable garden. The design is rustic yet elegant, with exposed brick walls, wooden beams, and antique furniture.

Why families love it: The hotel offers a “Little Farmer” program where kids can help plant vegetables, water the garden, and collect eggs from the chicken coop. The pool is surrounded by sun loungers and umbrellas, making it easy to supervise children while relaxing. The restaurant serves organic meals, with many ingredients sourced from the garden. The staff is incredibly patient and will happily prepare simple meals for picky eaters.

Pro tip: The hotel is located near several hiking trails suitable for families. The trail to Moon Hill is about 45 minutes and offers stunning views. Bring plenty of water and snacks.

10. Paddy Field Guesthouse: Simple Joys for Little Adventurers

Last but not least, Paddy Field Guesthouse is a budget-friendly option that does not compromise on family fun. Located in a rural area surrounded by rice paddies, this guesthouse is all about simple pleasures. The rooms are basic but clean, with mosquito nets and ceiling fans.

Why families love it: The guesthouse offers free use of bamboo rafts on the small canal behind the property. Kids love paddling around and spotting frogs and dragonflies. The owner’s children often play with guests, creating an instant playgroup. The communal dinners are a highlight, with everyone sitting around a large table sharing family-style dishes. The guesthouse also organizes night walks to spot frogs and listen to the sounds of the countryside.

Pro tip: The guesthouse is not suitable for families with very young babies, as there are no cribs or high chairs. It is best for families with children aged 4 and up.

What Makes Yangshuo Ideal for Family Travel?

Beyond the hotels, Yangshuo itself is a wonderland for children. The Li River is gentle and shallow in many sections, perfect for splashing and paddling. The karst mountains offer endless opportunities for gentle hikes and rock climbing (with professional guides). The local cuisine is mild and adaptable—most restaurants will happily prepare dishes without spice for children. And the pace of life is slow, allowing families to explore without feeling rushed.

The best time to visit is from March to November, with April, May, September, and October offering the most pleasant weather. Summer can be hot and humid, but the pools and rivers provide relief. Winter is cool and quiet, with fewer crowds and lower prices.

Final Thoughts on Family Travel in Yangshuo

Choosing the right hotel can make or break a family vacation. In Yangshuo, the options range from ultra-luxury resorts with kids’ clubs to rustic homestays where children can feed chickens and pick oranges. The key is to match your family’s style—do you want a structured program with activities and babysitting, or a more free-form adventure where every day is spontaneous?

No matter which hotel you choose, Yangshuo will leave your family with memories that last a lifetime. The sight of your child’s face lighting up as they paddle a bamboo raft for the first time, the sound of laughter echoing off the karst peaks, the taste of fresh lychees picked from a tree—these are the moments that make travel with children so rewarding. And in Yangshuo, those moments come easily, naturally, and often.
