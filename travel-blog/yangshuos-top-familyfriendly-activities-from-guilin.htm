Yangshuo’s Top Family-Friendly Activities from Guilin

Home / Travel Blog / Blog Details

If you are planning a trip to southern China with your kids, you have probably already heard about Guilin and its legendary karst landscape. But what many first-time visitors do not realize is that the real magic happens just an hour away in Yangshuo. This small county, surrounded by limestone peaks, winding rivers, and rice paddies, has become one of the most family-friendly destinations in all of Asia. And the best part? Getting there from Guilin is incredibly easy, whether by bus, boat, or high-speed train. In this guide, I will walk you through the very best things to do in Yangshuo with children, from gentle river floats to cooking classes, bike rides, and even a bit of culture. By the time you finish reading, you will be ready to pack your bags and book your tickets.

Getting from Guilin to Yangshuo with Kids

Before we dive into the activities, let us talk about logistics. The journey from Guilin to Yangshuo is part of the adventure. You have three main options, and each one offers a different experience.

The Li River Cruise: A Floating Fairy Tale

If you have the time and your children are old enough to sit still for a few hours, the Li River cruise is absolutely unforgettable. The boat ride takes about four to five hours, winding through the same karst peaks that appear on the back of the 20 yuan note. Kids will be mesmerized by the cormorant fishermen, the water buffalo wading in the shallows, and the sheer scale of the mountains rising straight out of the water. Most boats have air conditioning, snack bars, and restrooms, so even younger children can manage the trip if you bring plenty of distractions. Pro tip: book a morning departure so the light is soft and the heat is not yet intense.

The High-Speed Train: Fast and Efficient

For families with toddlers or infants, the high-speed train from Guilin to Yangshuo is a lifesaver. The trip takes only about 30 to 40 minutes, and the trains are clean, punctual, and have spacious aisles for strollers. The Yangshuo train station is located a bit outside the town center, but taxis and shuttle buses are readily available. This is by far the most stress-free option if your little ones get restless on long journeys.

Private Car or Taxi: Door-to-Door Comfort

If you are traveling with a lot of luggage or simply want the ultimate convenience, hiring a private car from Guilin to Yangshuo is very affordable by Western standards. The drive takes about 1.5 hours, and many drivers can provide car seats upon request. This option gives you the flexibility to stop for photos or snacks along the way, which is a huge plus when traveling with kids who need frequent breaks.

Top Family-Friendly Activities in Yangshuo

Now that you have arrived, let us get to the fun part. Yangshuo is packed with activities that cater to all ages. Whether your family loves outdoor adventures, cultural experiences, or just relaxing by a pool, you will find something here.

Biking Through the Countryside

One of the best ways to experience Yangshuo with kids is on two wheels. The countryside around the town is crisscrossed with paved paths and dirt trails that wind through rice paddies, past tiny villages, and alongside the Yulong River. Many rental shops offer bicycles with child seats, tandem bikes, or even electric bikes for older kids who want a little extra boost. The terrain is mostly flat, so even beginners can handle it.

Recommended Route: The Yulong River Trail

Start from the center of Yangshuo and head toward the Yulong River. This route takes you past the famous Moon Hill, through the village of Fuli, and along the riverbank where you can stop for a swim or a picnic. The total loop is about 15 kilometers, but you can easily shorten it by turning back whenever the kids get tired. Along the way, you will see farmers working in the fields, water buffalo cooling off in the mud, and local women washing clothes in the river. It is a living postcard.

Bamboo Rafting on the Yulong River

This is arguably the most popular family activity in Yangshuo, and for good reason. Unlike the noisy motorized rafts on the Li River, the bamboo rafts on the Yulong River are manually poled by a local guide. The ride is gentle, slow, and incredibly peaceful. Kids as young as one year old can safely sit on the raft, as long as they wear a life jacket. The rafts pass through small weirs where the water splashes gently, providing a little thrill without any danger. Most tours last about 1.5 hours, and you can choose from several different sections of the river. The section from the Yulong Bridge to the Gongnong Bridge is especially scenic and family-friendly.

What to Bring on the Raft

Sunscreen, hats, and water shoes are essential. The sun reflects off the water, and even on a cloudy day, you can get burned. Also, bring a waterproof bag for your phone and camera. You will want to capture the moment when your child reaches out to touch the cool water or waves at a passing cormorant.

Visiting the Yangshuo Park and West Street

Yangshuo town itself is small but charming. The Yangshuo Park is located right in the center and offers a peaceful escape from the bustle of West Street. The park has a small lake with paddle boats, a playground, and plenty of shaded benches. It is a great spot for a morning stroll or an afternoon picnic.

West Street, or Xi Jie, is the main pedestrian thoroughfare. It is lined with souvenir shops, street food stalls, and cafes. While it can get crowded in the evenings, it is perfectly safe and fun for kids. They will love the colorful lanterns, the live music, and the chance to try exotic snacks like grilled river fish or candied hawthorn skewers. Just keep a close eye on little ones in the crowd.

Cooking Classes for Families

If your kids are old enough to handle a knife (with supervision, of course), a family cooking class is a fantastic way to spend a few hours. Several schools in Yangshuo offer classes specifically designed for families. You will start with a trip to the local market, where your guide will explain the different vegetables, herbs, and spices used in Guangxi cuisine. Then, back in the kitchen, you will learn to make dishes like beer fish, stuffed tofu, and fried rice noodles. The best part is that you get to eat everything you cook. Many classes also offer vegetarian and kid-friendly options.

Why Cooking Classes Work for Kids

Cooking is hands-on, messy, and rewarding. It teaches kids about different cultures in a tangible way. Plus, picky eaters are more likely to try a dish they helped prepare. Many parents report that their children came home from Yangshuo with a new love for tofu or bok choy.

The Impression Sanjie Liu Light Show

This is a nighttime spectacle that will leave your entire family in awe. The show is performed on the Li River itself, with the karst mountains as a natural backdrop. Hundreds of performers, dressed in traditional costumes, dance, sing, and row boats under a canopy of lights. The show is based on a local folk tale and features music, choreography, and stunning visual effects. While it is a bit pricey, it is worth every penny. Kids as young as four or five will be captivated by the lights and the movement. Just bring a light jacket, as it can get cool by the water after sunset.

Rock Climbing for Older Kids and Teens

Yangshuo is one of the world’s top rock climbing destinations, and many climbing schools cater to beginners and children. If your kids are at least six or seven years old and have a sense of adventure, a half-day climbing session is an incredible experience. The routes are bolted and safe, and instructors speak English. The views from the top of a limestone cliff are absolutely breathtaking. Even if your child only makes it a few meters up, the sense of accomplishment is huge.

Best Climbing Spots for Families

Moon Hill is the most famous climbing spot, but it can be challenging. For families, I recommend the crags near the Yulong River or the ones just outside of town. These areas have easier routes and softer landings. Many climbing schools also offer bouldering options, which require no ropes and are perfect for younger kids.

Hot Air Balloon Rides

Yes, you read that correctly. Yangshuo has a hot air balloon operation that offers tethered rides over the countryside. The balloon does not fly free; it is attached to a rope and goes up to about 100 meters. But even from that height, the views are spectacular. Kids love the sensation of floating upward, and the sheer size of the balloon is impressive. The rides are short, usually about 10 to 15 minutes, which is perfect for short attention spans. Make sure to book in advance, as slots fill up quickly during peak season.

Visiting Local Villages

If you want to get off the beaten path, take a day trip to one of the nearby villages. Fuli Village is famous for its handmade fans, and you can watch artisans painting and assembling them. Xingping Village is the place where the iconic 20 yuan note photo was taken, and it offers a quieter, more authentic glimpse of rural life. In these villages, kids can see how locals live, interact with friendly farmers, and even try their hand at traditional crafts. Most villages are within a 30-minute drive from Yangshuo, and many tour operators offer half-day excursions.

Where to Stay in Yangshuo with Kids

Choosing the right accommodation can make or break a family trip. Yangshuo has a wide range of options, from budget hostels to luxury resorts. For families, I recommend staying in a hotel or guesthouse that offers family rooms, a swimming pool, and easy access to the town center.

Family-Friendly Hotels

The Yangshuo Mountain Retreat is a popular choice for families. It is located along the Yulong River, has a large pool, and offers bike rentals and rafting packages. The staff is incredibly helpful and can arrange activities tailored to your family’s interests. Another great option is the Banyan Tree Yangshuo, which is a bit more upscale but offers spacious villas, a kids’ club, and a spa. If you are on a budget, the Giggling Tree Hostel has family rooms and a laid-back atmosphere that many parents love.

What to Look for in a Family Hotel

When booking, look for properties that offer cribs or rollaway beds, have a restaurant with kid-friendly menu options, and are located near the main attractions. Also, check if the hotel provides babysitting services. Even a few hours of adult time can be a lifesaver on a long trip.

Eating with Kids in Yangshuo

One of the biggest challenges of traveling with children is finding food they will actually eat. In Yangshuo, you will be pleasantly surprised. The local cuisine is not as spicy as Sichuan or Hunan food, and many dishes are mild and comforting.

Kid-Friendly Dishes to Try

Beer fish is the signature dish of Yangshuo. It is a whole fish braised in beer, ginger, and soy sauce, then served in a clay pot. The sauce is savory and slightly sweet, and most kids love it. Another winner is stir-fried rice noodles, which are similar to pad Thai but lighter. For picky eaters, most restaurants offer fried rice, steamed vegetables, and simple noodle soups. Western food is also widely available, especially on West Street. You will find pizza, pasta, burgers, and even tacos.

Street Food Safety

Street food is a big part of the Yangshuo experience, but you need to be careful with young children. Stick to stalls that are busy and have high turnover. Avoid anything that has been sitting out for a long time. Grilled items, like corn on the cob or skewered meats, are generally safe because they are cooked to order. Fresh fruit is also a great option, especially mangoes and dragon fruit.

Practical Tips for Families

A few final pieces of advice to ensure your trip goes smoothly.

Weather and Packing

Yangshuo has a subtropical climate, with hot, humid summers and mild winters. The best time to visit with kids is spring (March to May) or autumn (September to November) when the weather is pleasant and the crowds are smaller. Pack lightweight clothing, comfortable walking shoes, rain jackets, and plenty of sunscreen. Mosquito repellent is also a must, especially in the evenings.

Health and Safety

Yangshuo is a very safe destination for families. The locals are friendly and used to tourists. However, you should still take standard precautions. Keep an eye on your children near water and on busy streets. Make sure everyone stays hydrated, and carry a basic first-aid kit. Tap water is not drinkable, so stick to bottled water for drinking and brushing teeth.

Language Barrier

English is widely spoken in tourist areas, but it is always helpful to learn a few basic phrases in Mandarin. “Xie xie” (thank you) and “ni hao” (hello) will go a long way. Many hotels and restaurants have English menus, and tour guides are usually bilingual.

Getting Around

Within Yangshuo town, walking is the easiest way to get around. For trips to the countryside, you can rent a bike, hire a taxi, or join a guided tour. Many hotels offer shuttle services to popular attractions. If you are using ride-hailing apps, Didi is the most reliable option.

A Sample 5-Day Family Itinerary

To help you plan, here is a sample itinerary that balances activity with relaxation.

Day 1: Arrival and Exploration Arrive in Yangshuo from Guilin via high-speed train. Check into your hotel and spend the afternoon exploring West Street and Yangshuo Park. Have dinner at a local restaurant and try the beer fish.

Day 2: River Adventure Take a bamboo raft ride on the Yulong River in the morning. After lunch, rent bikes and ride along the Yulong River trail. Stop for a swim if the weather is warm. In the evening, watch the Impression Sanjie Liu show.

Day 3: Culture and Cooking Visit Fuli Village in the morning to see fan making. In the afternoon, take a family cooking class. Eat your creations for dinner.

Day 4: Adventure Day Go rock climbing in the morning (for older kids) or visit Moon Hill for a gentle hike. In the afternoon, take a hot air balloon ride. Spend the evening relaxing at your hotel’s pool.

Day 5: Departure Enjoy a leisurely breakfast, then take a private car back to Guilin for your flight or train.

Why Yangshuo is Perfect for Families

Yangshuo offers something that few destinations can match: a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural richness, and family-friendly activities. It is not too crowded, not too expensive, and not too far from Guilin. The pace of life is slow, the people are warm, and the scenery is nothing short of magical. Whether your kids are toddlers or teenagers, they will find something to love here. And you, as a parent, will get to relax and enjoy the journey too. So go ahead, book that trip. The karst mountains are waiting.
